What’s inside

Six apps that share one window, one search (Ctrl+K) and one place for your files.

DocsHeadings, lists, tables, images and links. Opens Word, HTML, text and Markdown files; saves as Word, HTML, text or PDF.
SheetsFormulas such as SUM, AVERAGE, IF and COUNTIF, several sheets per file, sort and filter. Opens and saves CSV and Excel.
FormsNine question types. Collect answers on your computer, see them as charts, or open them in Sheets.
PDFAdd text and dates, sign or initial, fill a PDF’s own form fields, highlight, redact, merge and reorder pages.
TrackerTasks with due dates and priority, daily habits with streaks, and a short note for each day.
FilesEvery file you’ve made or opened, with search. Drop files onto the window to open them.
Works offlineFiles are stored on your computer. There’s no Forma account or server.
Updates itselfInstall once. Each update is signed and only installs if the signature checks out.
No telemetryNo analytics or crash reports. The only network request is the update check.
Open sourceMIT licensed. Read the code, report bugs or send improvements.
